# RMW profitability

At each June formation date, operating profitability is annual revenue minus COGS, SG&A and interest expense, divided by positive book equity for the fiscal year ending in the prior calendar year. The statement must have been public by formation. Independent NYSE median size and 30/70 profitability breakpoints form six portfolios held July through June; RMW is the average robust-minus-weak spread across small and big stocks. Monthly portfolio returns use lagged market-equity weights.
