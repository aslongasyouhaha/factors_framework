# Net operating assets

`NOA = [(AT-CHE) - (AT-DLC-DLTT-MIB-PSTK-CEQ)] / lagged AT`. At each June, the prior-calendar-year fiscal statement must already be public. Financial firms are excluded. Independent NYSE size and signed-NOA 30/70 breakpoints form six portfolios held July through June; the reported factor is low minus high NOA with lagged-market-equity weights.
